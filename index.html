Questions & answers

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. No installation of extra runtimes needed — download, run, capture.

Do my screenshots get uploaded anywhere?

Never. MarkMyShot makes zero network connections. Screenshots are saved to a folder you choose on your own disk, and that's the only place they exist.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

You keep full access to viewing, editing and copying your existing screenshots. Only capturing new ones requires a license — your work is never held hostage.
